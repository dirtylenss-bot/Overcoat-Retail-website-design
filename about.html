About Overcoats

The Overcoat Importer is South Africa's leading importer of worn overcoats. We pride ourselves in having the largest selection of top quality coats imported from Europe for the entire family. Our coats are sold individually so you could find your perfect coat at any one of our stores, conveniently located within comfortable walking distance from the train and taxi transport routes.

The Overcoat Importer creates opportunities for entrepreneurs with the buy-a-bale, make your own cash concept. Bales of coats are available at wholesale prices from Overcoats Benoni, Coat Corner Kempton Park and Coat Corner Springs for collection or countrywide delivery.

Great care is taken when packaging the bales for the wholesale market so that our customers always get the best value for money. Our highly experienced and motivated sales team is ready to assist and offer the best advice possible.
